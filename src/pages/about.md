## What PH Navigator is

PH Navigator is an independent project to give people with pulmonary hypertension (PH), and the people who care for them, clear and practical information in plain language. It focuses on Australia and New Zealand.

## Where it's up to

This site is an **early preview**. We have started with the essentials for someone who has recently been diagnosed:

- [What PH is](/what-is-ph/)
- [How it's diagnosed](/diagnosis-and-tests/)
- [Questions to ask your doctor](/questions-for-your-doctor/)
- [An overview of treatments](/treatments/)
- [Hospitals with PH services](/find-a-specialist/)

More topics, such as living well with PH and support for carers, will be added once the core pages have been reviewed.

## How health information is checked

None of the health information on this site has been clinically reviewed yet. Every page with health information shows its review status at the top, and will show who reviewed it and when once that has happened.

We are in discussions with the Pulmonary Hypertension Society of Australia and New Zealand (PHSANZ) about clinical review. PHSANZ has not reviewed or endorsed this site.
