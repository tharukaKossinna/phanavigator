## At your first appointment or diagnosis

- What type (group) of pulmonary hypertension do I have?
- What is the underlying cause, if known?
- Are my symptoms typical for my stage of the condition?
- What are the goals of my treatment?
- Who will be my main point of contact, for example a PH nurse?

## About treatment

- What medicines are you recommending, and how do they work?
- What side effects could I have, and what should I do if I get them?
- Will these interact with other medicines or supplements I take?
- Do I need oxygen therapy now, or might I in the future?
- How soon will we know if the treatment is working?

## Daily life

- Should I limit salt or fluids?
- What kind of exercise is safe for me? Should I do pulmonary rehabilitation?
- Are there limits on travel, especially flying or going to high altitudes?
- Are there support groups or psychological support services you recommend?
