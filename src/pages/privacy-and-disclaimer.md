## Medical disclaimer

PH Navigator provides general information only. It is not medical advice, and it does not replace advice from your doctor or PH specialist team, who know your situation.

- Never start, stop or change a medicine without talking to your doctor first.
- If you are unwell or your symptoms change, contact your doctor or PH team.
- In an emergency, call **000** in Australia or **111** in New Zealand.

The health information on this site has not yet been clinically reviewed. Each page shows its review status at the top.

## Privacy

This site does not ask for any personal information. It has no forms, accounts, newsletter, cookies, tracking or analytics, and it does not load fonts, videos or other content from third-party services.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

Some pages link to other websites, such as hospital websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

If we add anything that collects information in future, such as a newsletter, we will update this page first.
